05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you choose,
a few useful answers.

Which broadband plan is right for me?

Basic offers 50 Mbps at ₹499 + GST/month; Standard offers 100 Mbps at ₹899 + GST/month; Business offers 200 Mbps at ₹1,299 + GST/month; Entertainment offers 300 Mbps at ₹1,699 + GST/month. Your ideal choice depends on your devices and usage.

How do I get a new connection?

Select a plan and submit the enquiry form with your name, phone number and location. Complete the spam check if prompted. The HAS Networks team can then review your enquiry and confirm coverage and installation.

Can I watch OTT content on my TV?

Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5, Sun NXT and YuppTV are included in all four plans. Netflix is also included in the ₹899, ₹1,299 and ₹1,699 plans, plus GST. App activation, device access and usage follow the relevant provider’s terms; ask us about the setup for your TV.

Is broadband available in my area?

Enter your city and PIN code in the enquiry form. The HAS Networks team will need to confirm coverage for your exact address.
