1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of information a website or its service providers may store in your browser. Similar browser storage can also be used for preferences, security or tracking.

2. Current website features

The current HAS Networks website code does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not use local storage to save enquiry details. Slideshow state is held in page memory and resets when the page is reloaded. Submitting an enquiry takes you to FormSubmit for processing and spam verification. FormSubmit and its verification providers, including Google reCAPTCHA, may use their own cookies or similar technologies on their pages. See FormSubmit’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Hosting and Google Fonts

The hosting platform may use its own security, access or operational technologies. These depend on the platform on which the site is deployed. Google Fonts is requested to display the typefaces; this involves external font requests and technical connection information. Read Google’s Privacy Policy for more information.

4. Advertising and analytics

Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, AdSense and remarketing are not installed in the current site code. If such services are added, the business must first update these disclosures and implement consent choices where required before loading optional tracking technologies.

5. Your controls

You can review, clear or block cookies using your browser settings. Blocking hosting-platform security or access cookies may affect access to a hosted site. You can also control downloaded enquiry files directly on your device.
