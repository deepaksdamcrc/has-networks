QUESTIONS WE CAN HELP WITH

Good to know.

Which speeds can I choose?

The current home plans are 50 Mbps at ₹499 + GST, 100 Mbps at ₹899 + GST, 200 Mbps at ₹1,299 + GST and 300 Mbps at ₹1,699 + GST per month. All plans include Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5, Sun NXT and YuppTV. Netflix is included from the ₹899 + GST plan; Basic does not include Netflix. Confirm activation, taxes, equipment and current availability before ordering.

Will Wi-Fi reach every room?

Walls, distance and router placement affect Wi-Fi. Tell us about your layout so we can discuss placement or whether extra equipment is needed.
