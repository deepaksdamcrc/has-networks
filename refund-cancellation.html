Refund & Cancellation

How to raise a cancellation or payment-related request.

Updated: 18 November 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsRefund & CancellationCookie Policy

1. Website enquiries

Submitting an enquiry through this website is free. The current website does not collect payments, take deposits or activate subscriptions. You can abandon an enquiry without a website cancellation charge.

2. Existing connections and paid orders

If you have paid HAS Networks or already have a connection, cancellation and refund eligibility depend on your accepted service agreement, the payment or charge concerned, services already delivered and applicable law. This page does not impose a blanket “no refund” rule or promise an automatic refund.

3. How to request a review

Call +91 8534 - 946 - 946 or contact HAS Networks at the address below. Provide the account holder’s name, registered phone number, relevant bill or payment reference, payment date, amount and the reason for your request. Never share passwords, OTPs or full payment-card details.

4. Charges and equipment

Ask the team to confirm whether any installation, activation, recurring service, equipment, deposit or return conditions apply to your specific agreement. Do not assume a charge is refundable or non-refundable without checking the terms supplied for your order.

5. Review and resolution

HAS Networks will need to review the relevant order, payment and service records to determine the applicable outcome. Ask for the confirmed outcome, any approved refund amount, payment method and expected processing time for your case. No fixed refund or cancellation turnaround is stated on this website.

6. Payment issues and consumer rights

For suspected duplicate, failed or incorrect payments, contact us with the transaction reference so the records can be checked. This policy does not limit any refund, complaint or other remedy available under applicable law.
