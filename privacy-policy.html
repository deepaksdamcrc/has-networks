Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this website.

1. Who we are

This policy describes information handling for this HAS Networks broadband website. Our business contact details are provided below.

2. Information entered in the enquiry form

Depending on the form you use, we may ask for your name, mobile number, city or area, PIN code, location details you provide, chosen service or plan, contact permission and a message describing your enquiry or support issue. When you submit it, the details are sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service, for processing and delivery to HAS Networks at deepak09kumaar@gmail.com, with a copy to hasnetworks1@gmail.com. The website itself does not maintain an enquiry database. Alternatively, “Email us directly” opens your email application with the enquiry details and both recipients. That alternative is sent only when you choose to send the message in your email application.

We use the information you submit to assess availability, answer your enquiry and discuss a connection. The form requires your permission to contact you about that enquiry. You may instead contact us by phone or email. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Website delivery and external services

Your hosting provider may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, requested pages, device or browser information and security logs to deliver and protect the website. This website also requests font files from Google Fonts; these requests share technical connection information such as your IP address with Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy for its practices.

FormSubmit processes submitted information and technical connection details for email delivery and spam prevention. Its verification screen may use Google reCAPTCHA. See FormSubmit’s Privacy Policy for its practices. Submitted details also pass through the email providers used to deliver and receive messages, including Gmail.

The current website code does not include Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, AdSense advertisements or remarketing tags. Those services and their associated advertising consent requirements are not active on this website.

4. Use and disclosure

Information you voluntarily share directly with HAS Networks may be used for customer enquiries, service availability, connection arrangements and support. Where needed for a requested service, information may be shared with relevant service providers or as required by law. The enquiry form is used to route service enquiries, not to enrol you in advertising campaigns.

5. Retention and your choices

Enquiries may be retained in the receiving mailboxes and processed by the submission and email providers under their own retention practices. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information held by HAS Networks. Information needed for an existing service, billing, dispute resolution or legal obligations may need to be retained.

6. Security and children

Use a trusted device and share your enquiry only with the intended recipient. No online system or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure. The enquiry form is intended for people arranging a broadband or business service, not for children submitting personal information independently.

7. Changes

This notice should be updated when changing enquiry providers or introducing payments, analytics or advertising. The updated date at the top identifies this version.
